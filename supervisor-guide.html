Supervisor guide

Eligibility

Requirements for taking on MMed dissertation supervision, from the MOU through examination.

  • Hold an MMed, Master's or equivalent, or demonstrate equivalent research standing through a peer-reviewed publication record.
  • Provide discipline-specific expertise; appoint a co-supervisor where additional expertise is needed.
  • Be available for regular meetings and timely feedback.
  • Limited prior supervision experience → complete a Faculty-approved supervisor training course.
  • External primary supervisor → a UCT-based co-supervisor with a valid UCT staff number must be appointed.
  • Appointments and changes are approved by the HOD and recorded on the MOU / Change of Supervisor form.
Thesis stage

Nominating examiners

  • Submit at least two nominations: one external to UCT, one internal and independent of the project.
  • For each: name and title, position and affiliation, contact details, and a concise summary of expertise and relevance.
  • Avoid conflicts of interest — recent co-authorship, shared funding, close collaboration or personal connections.
  • The supervisor may check availability only; the Faculty Office formally invites examiners and manages all correspondence.
  • Examiner identities are confidential from the student at all stages. Breaches can delay graduation and trigger formal Faculty review.
Reference

Supervisor FAQs

Is ethics approval needed for all MMed research? All research involving human participants, material or data requires HREC oversight — approval, waiver or exemption. Database and folder-review studies still go through DRC then HREC.

Must HREC approval be current at thesis submission? Yes — the thesis will not proceed to examination without evidence of current approval or renewal.

When is D8 due? At least six weeks before the intended submission date.

Who contacts examiners? The supervisor may establish willingness only; all subsequent contact is via the Faculty Office.

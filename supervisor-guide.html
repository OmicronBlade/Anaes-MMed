Roles and responsibilities

From the Department's MMed Registrar Administrative and Research Progression Guide (draft, 23 Aug 2026) — a departmental draft awaiting sign-off, not yet final policy. It sets out who is expected to do what across the MMed's four-year progression.

Registrar

  • Maintain annual UCT registration and respond to administrative requests within deadlines.
  • Identify a research question and supervisor early, and sign the research MOU.
  • Arrange and attend supervision meetings; keep a written record of agreed actions and deadlines.
  • Complete protocol, ethics and institutional approvals before data collection.
  • Maintain the agreed research timeline and submit progress reports when requested.
  • Keep the portfolio/logbook and academic requirements up to date.
  • Escalate barriers early rather than waiting until a deadline has been missed.

Research Supervisor

  • Assist with project scope, feasibility, protocol development, ethics submission, analysis and writing.
  • Agree a realistic work plan with the registrar and document progress against it.
  • Meet the registrar at a frequency appropriate to the project, with a minimum documented progress review at least every six months.
  • Complete the supervisor component of the research MOU and periodic research progress reports.
  • Alert the MMed/Research Lead early if progress is persistently below the agreed plan.

MMed / Research Lead

  • Orientate new registrars to the MMed pathway and expectations.
  • Track departmental milestones, identify delayed projects and coordinate support/remediation.
  • Facilitate departmental research review before ethics submission.
  • Provide a clear escalation route for unresolved supervisor or project problems.
  • Confirm that departmental academic requirements are met before recommending final-examination suitability.

Departmental Administrator

  • Maintain registration and progression records supplied by UCT and the Department.
  • Track signed MOUs, progress reports and required administrative submissions.
  • Send reminders for annual registration, HPCSA paperwork and departmental deadlines.
  • Escalate missing documentation to the registrar and programme leadership.

Head of Department (Programme Convener)

  • Approve departmental MOU/progression arrangements where required.
  • Provide or withhold departmental suitability/support in accordance with published UCT/CMSA rules and departmental requirements.
  • Ensure that concerns about progress are managed through a fair, documented and consistent process.
Supervisor guide

Eligibility

Requirements for taking on MMed dissertation supervision, from the MOU through examination.

  • Hold an MMed, Master's or equivalent, or demonstrate equivalent research standing through a peer-reviewed publication record.
  • Provide discipline-specific expertise; appoint a co-supervisor where additional expertise is needed.
  • Be available for regular meetings and timely feedback — a documented progress review with your registrar at least every six months is departmental draft policy. DRAFT POLICY
  • Limited prior supervision experience → complete a Faculty-approved supervisor training course.
  • External primary supervisor → a UCT-based co-supervisor with a valid UCT staff number must be appointed.
  • Appointments and changes are approved by the HOD and recorded on the MOU / Change of Supervisor form.
Thesis stage

Nominating examiners

  • Submit at least two nominations: one external to UCT, one internal and independent of the project.
  • For each: name and title, position and affiliation, contact details, and a concise summary of expertise and relevance.
  • Avoid conflicts of interest — recent co-authorship, shared funding, close collaboration or personal connections.
  • The supervisor may check availability only; the Faculty Office formally invites examiners and manages all correspondence.
  • Examiner identities are confidential from the student at all stages. Breaches can delay graduation and trigger formal Faculty review.

Supervisor FAQs

Is ethics approval needed for all MMed research? All research involving human participants, material or data requires HREC oversight — approval, waiver or exemption. Database and folder-review studies still go through DRC then HREC.

Must HREC approval be current at thesis submission? Yes — the thesis will not proceed to examination without evidence of current approval or renewal.

When is D8 due? At least six weeks before the intended submission date.

Who contacts examiners? The supervisor may establish willingness only; all subsequent contact is via the Faculty Office.

Is there an expected timeline for the MMed? The Department's draft progression guide proposes a semester-by-semester trajectory over four years — see the full Expected progression timeline on the Student Research Journey page. DRAFT POLICY

What happens if a registrar's progress is unsatisfactory? The draft departmental approach is early support and a documented recovery plan with revised dates first. Escalation through departmental and Faculty academic progression processes is reserved for persistent failure to progress despite documented feedback, reasonable support and a remediation plan. DRAFT POLICY

What if a registrar leaves the post before finishing the MMed? Continued academic registration and completion are governed by current UCT rules; the registrar remains responsible for maintaining communication with the supervisor and Department and complying with an agreed completion plan.
